Interactive Historical Atlas

Welcome to Biblical World Explorer

Designed for students, pastors, historians, and readers of Scripture, this atlas connects biblical texts directly to the physical topography, hydrology, and historical boundaries of the Ancient Near East and Mediterranean world.

1. 3D Globe & Relief Map Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag (or Shift+drag) to tilt. Switch between 3D Globe, 3D Regional Relief, and 2D Flat Atlas.
2. Chapter-by-Chapter Analysis Explore Genesis through Acts. Chapters without explicit geographical references are transparently flagged rather than assumed.
3. Four-Tier Epistemic Rigor Every site profile distinguishes Scripture, geographical evidence, archaeological findings, and scholarly uncertainties.
4. 3D Event Terrain Reconstructions Inspect interactive 3D topographic models of key biblical events—clearly labeled as scholarly reconstructions.
