Welcome to Biblical World Explorer

Every story in Scripture happened in real mountains, valleys, rivers, and cities. This atlas lets you explore all 66 books and 1,189 chapters in interactive 3D—no geography background needed.

1. Pick Any Chapter (Genesis 1 – Revelation 22) Open the Chapters tab to select any book and chapter. Even poetic or letter chapters show where they were written or set, plus an interactive 3D Scene.
2. Rotate, Tilt & Zoom in 3D Drag to rotate the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom, and switch between 3D Globe, 3D Relief, and 2D Atlas at the top of the map.
3. Listen & Ask Questions Tap Listen on any chapter for a spoken audio walkthrough, or open Ask Guide to ask questions about distances, elevations, and history.
